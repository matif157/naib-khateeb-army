# Naib Khateeb (نائب خطیب) — Pakistan Army

A compiled research report on the **Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO) — Naib Khateeb**
recruitment in the Pakistan Army: eligibility, selection criteria, test pattern,
training, pay, plus an honest reality analysis of merit, competition, and chances.

> Sources: pakarmy.com.pk, propakistani.pk, todaygovtjobs.com, shaheenleaderacademy.com,
> APP / The Nation, joinpakarmy.gov.pk. Figures change every recruitment cycle — always
> verify against the **current official advertisement** at <https://www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk>.

---

## 1. What It Is
- **Position:** Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO) — religious scholar / sermon deputy.
- **Branch:** Pakistan Army (a separate similar branch exists in the Pakistan Navy).
- **Title meaning:** "Naib" = deputy/assistant; "Khateeb" = one who delivers the Khutbah (sermon). Reports to the **Khateeb**.
- **Status:** Regular JCO recruitment (not a commissioned officer rank).
- **Gender:** Male only. **Nationality:** Pakistan / AJK citizen.

## 2. Role & Responsibilities
- Leads daily prayers (Imamat) for troops in units/garrisons.
- Delivers Friday (Jumu'ah) sermons and religious lectures.
- Teaches Islamic values and Qur'an; provides religious education.
- Offers spiritual/moral counseling to soldiers; supports discipline, morale, and ethics.
- Assists in garrison/field religious duties.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Education | **Dars-e-Nizami (8-year course)** / Shahadat-ul-Aalamiyyah from HEC-recognized board **+ FA/FSc** or equivalent |
| Age | **20–28 years** (some sources 21–28); as on advertised cut-off date |
| Serving Army personnel | **22–35 years**, with 1–16 years' service (via proper channels) |
| Marital status | Married or Unmarried |
| Gender | Male |
| Height | Minimum **5'3" (160 cm)** |
| Preferred / advantage | **Hafiz-e-Quran**; Arabic speaking, **Qira'at**, **Tajweed**, Hifz; BA/MA Islamic Studies/Arabic |
| Note | Candidates lacking strong Qur'anic/Arabic/Tajweed knowledge are **not eligible** |

## 4. Age & Height Relaxations
- **Balochistan & merged KP districts (FATA):** up to **+3 years age**; **2-inch height** relaxation.
- **Higher qualifications (graduates):** up to **+1 year age** (verify for JCO).
- **Children of Shuhada (martyrs) / war-wounded, sons of serving/retired personnel:** concessions per Army regulations.
- Height relaxation does **not** apply to certain trades (Clerks, Military Police, Cook, Sanitary Worker).

## 5. Selection Process (Step-by-Step)
1. **Registration** — online at `www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk` **OR** walk-in form at nearest **Army Selection & Recruitment Centre (AS&RC)**. (For Naib Khateeb, form submission at AS&RC is required; online not compulsory.)
2. **Initial Screening / Document Check** — verify age, height, education.
3. **Physical Test** — running, push-ups, sit-ups, chin-ups, ditch crossing (see §6).
4. **Initial Medical Examination** — height/weight/BMI, eyesight, etc.
5. **Written Test** — intelligence + academic (see §7).
6. **Interview / Religious Assessment** — Qur'an recitation, Arabic, Islamic knowledge, personality (see §8).
7. **Final Medical** — full examination (e.g., at CMH).
8. **Merit & Final Selection** — GHQ recommendation; merit list based on academics + written + physical + interview.

## 6. Physical Standards
| Test | Standard |
|---|---|
| Run | **1.6 km (1 mile)** — sources vary: **8–9 minutes** |
| Push-ups | 15 reps in 2 minutes |
| Sit-ups | 15–20 reps in 2 minutes |
| Chin-ups | 3 reps in 2 minutes |
| Ditch crossing | 7'4" × 7'4" |

## 7. Written Test Pattern
**Intelligence portion** (per pakarmy.com.pk):
| Test | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal Intelligence | ~65 | 30 min |
| Non-Verbal Intelligence | ~75 | 35 min |

- **Verbal:** series completion (number/letter), coding-decoding, analogies, direction sense, ranking/time, age relationships, percentage.
- **Non-verbal:** series/pattern continuation, classification, odd-one-out, figure rotation, analogies.

**Academic portion** (varies by source; typically):
- English language & grammar
- Urdu language
- Basic mathematics & reasoning
- **Islamic Studies:** Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh, Sirah, pillars of Islam, general Islamic knowledge
- General knowledge / Pakistan studies

*(Exact academic subject weightage for Naib Khateeb is not published uniformly — verify against the current official advertisement.)*

## 8. Interview / Religious Assessment
Candidates who clear written + physical + medical are interviewed at AS&RC:
- **Qur'an recitation** — accuracy, pronunciation (Tajweed), fluency
- **Arabic language** — comprehension, vocabulary, communication
- **Islamic knowledge** — Hadith, Fiqh, general Islamic studies
- **Personality & discipline** — motivation, confidence, behavior, suitability for army life

## 9. Training (Post-Selection)
- **Basic Military Training (BMT):** discipline, drill, weapons handling, physical fitness
- **Religious Role Training:** delivering sermons, teaching, Qur'anic guidance, Islamic counseling
- **Field & Garrison Duties:** daily garrison duties, assisting soldiers
- **Continuous evaluation** throughout training

## 10. Rank, Pay & Career Progression
- **Initial rank:** Junior Commissioned Officer (entry JCO).
- **JCO rank ladder (Army general):** Naib Subedar (BPS-14) → Subedar (BPS-15) → Subedar Major (BPS-16).
- **Approx. salary:** **Rs. 60,000+/month** (entry; incl. allowances).
- **Service duration:** typically **20–30 years**.
- **Promotions:** eligible for higher JCO ranks based on performance and seniority.

## 11. Benefits
- Attractive salary + allowances; pension; medical facilities
- Free accommodation and ration
- Subsidized education for children
- Job security and respect

## 12. Required Documents (at AS&RC)
- Original educational certificates, DMC, degrees (+ 2 attested copies)
- CNIC (18+) or Smart B-Form (under 18); Father's/Guardian's CNIC
- Original Domicile Certificate + PRC

- Hafiz-e-Quran certificate (if applicable)

## 16. Reality Analysis — Merit, Safarish & Realistic Chances

### Profile vs. what the job actually tests
The role is a **public reciter and prayer-leader**: core daily job is **leading prayers (Imamat), reciting Qur'an aloud, and delivering Friday Khutbah**. Qirat/Tajweed is the job, not a bonus.

| Assessed area | Typical candidate level | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Qirat / Tajweed | Required & directly tested | Critical |
| Hifz (memorization) | Strongly preferred | High |
| Deep Islamic knowledge (Hadith, Fiqh, Sirah) | Tested at interview | High |
| Arabic communication | Tested | Medium |
| Nahw/Sarf (gardan, tarkeeb) | Useful | Medium |
| Tarjuma of ayats | Useful | Medium |
| English communication | Written test advantage | Medium |
| Dars-e-Nizami (bare pass) | Doesn't disqualify, no merit edge | Low |

### Reality of "merit" in this branch
- Intake is **small**, but the pool contains **Huffaz, Qaris, and Fazil/Mufti-level scholars**.
- Officially merit-based; the religious interview judges live recitation — **harder to game** than many other trades.
- Practically, **merit is measured on Qirat + Islamic depth**.

### The safarish reality
- Influence exists across Pakistani govt recruitment, but **a retired parent's safarish cannot override a hard requirement like live Qur'an recitation**.
- At most it can (a) ensure fair handling, (b) give a **marginal push if already borderline**.
- Over-reaching safarish can **backfire** in Army entries.
- Best use of contacts: **insider guidance** (current AS&RC expectations, interview focus, timing).

### Realistic probability by stage (subjective estimate)
| Stage | Odds (weak-Qirat candidate now) |
|---|---|
| Eligible (docs: FSc + Dars-e-Nizami) | High (if madrassa recognized) |
| Written / intelligence test | Moderate–High |
| Physical + medical | Depends on fitness |
| Religious interview / Qirat | Low |
| Final merit selection | Low–Moderate (only if Qirat fixed) |

**Conclusion:** passing early stages is plausible; being selected *now* with weak Qirat is unlikely. With focused Qirat training (and ideally starting Hifz) before applying, odds move to genuinely realistic.

### What changes the result (priority order)
1. **Fix Qirat/Tajweed first** — the single biggest gap; directly tested.
2. **Start Hifz (even partial)** — earns the documented Hafiz preference.
3. **Learn the practical job** — correct Salah, Jumu'ah Khutbah structure, common duas.
4. **Deepen core Islamic knowledge** — Fiqh of worship, basic Hadith, Sirah, aqeedah.
5. **Arabic speaking/understanding** — convert "can learn" to actual ability.
6. **Upgrade education** — BA/MA Islamic Studies or Arabic (merit + long-term promotion).
7. **Train the intelligence test + physical.**
8. **Use contacts for guidance, not nepotism.**

### Backup routes
- **Join as a soldier now**, then apply as **serving personnel (age 22–35)** later (internal advantage; English helps for Clerk trade).
- **Parallel religious posts:** Pak Navy Naib Khateeb; PAF Religious Teacher / CRT; **ASF Religious Teacher (BPS-08, Shahadat-ul-Almia + Hafiz with Tajweed)**; provincial Islamic-studies teaching.
- **Madrassa / Islamic-school teaching** using grammar + tarjuma + English.
